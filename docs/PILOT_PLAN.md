# Design-partner pilot plan

## Objective

Determine whether Salesforce users repeatedly value structural email review enough to keep it in their workflow.

## Cohort

Target:
- 3 organizations;
- 5–15 users per organization;
- at least one internal champion and one Salesforce admin contact per organization.

## Duration

Four weeks.

### Week 0 — setup
- confirm scope;
- confirm privacy/data handling;
- deploy or provision;
- train users in under 20 minutes;
- capture baseline workflow.

### Week 1 — activation
Measure:
- invited users;
- activated users;
- reviews started;
- composer handoffs.

### Week 2 — repeat use
Measure:
- repeat users;
- reviews per active user;
- findings opened/dismissed;
- qualitative annoyance points.

### Week 3 — mode comparison
Compare:
- Review-first;
- Live Coach.

Ask:
- which mode is preferred;
- whether Live Coach interrupts thinking;
- whether Review-first arrives too late.

### Week 4 — continuation
Ask:
- would the team continue using it;
- who would own budget;
- what security/procurement blockers remain;
- willingness to discuss a paid pilot.

## Internal success gates

These are product-development gates, not marketing claims.

- ≥60% repeat usage among activated users;
- ≥40% suggestion interaction/action rate;
- ≥2 of 3 organizations request continued or commercial access.

## Research questions

- Which structural findings are genuinely useful?
- Which are obvious or annoying?
- Does the user want suggestions or one-click fixes?
- Does the tool belong in Salesforce or in the email client?
- Which CRM records should ground the review?
- Does recognized structured-writing methodology materially increase trust?
- Who approves installation?
- Who pays?

## Data rule

Collect event-level telemetry by default.

Do not collect raw message content unless the pilot organization separately and explicitly approves research sampling.
